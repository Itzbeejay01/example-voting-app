# Robot Shop availability SLO

SLI: successful synthetic HTTP probes to `web.robot-shop.svc.cluster.local:8080/` divided by all blackbox probes, where a non-2xx response or connection failure is unsuccessful. Target: 99.0% over a rolling 30-day window. This measures service reachability from Prometheus inside the cluster, not end-user request volume. The monthly error budget is 1%, equal to 432 minutes of unavailability in a 30-day month. Alert when five-minute probe availability falls below 99% for ten minutes. Planned maintenance counts unless announced as an excluded training window. The owner reviews budget weekly; feature releases pause when the budget is exhausted. Calculate consumed budget as `(1 - observed 30-day availability) / 0.01`; at 98.5% availability, 50% of the budget is consumed.
